01 · About

Twenty years between business problems and the models that solve them.

I started as a business analyst and product owner in telecom, sitting with customers to turn messy operational needs into working systems. That habit stayed with me when I moved into data science: start from the decision someone needs to make, then build the model, the pipeline and the evaluation that make it reliable.

Today I lead end to end AI solutions at Microsoft, from problem framing and experimentation through evaluation, deployment and adoption, currently for healthcare and life sciences customers. My research background in graphs, knowledge graphs, graph embeddings and graph neural networks shapes how I approach retrieval, reasoning and knowledge discovery.

  • Based in Israel
  • Arabic, Hebrew and English
  • Microsoft Global Hackathon winner three years running, 2024 to 2026
  • PhD, Ariel University · MSc, University of Haifa

Good AI is not a demo. It is a system people trust with real decisions: measured, evaluated and running in production.
